Why almost every habit tracker is a subscription

Search the App Store for a habit tracker and you'll notice a pattern. The download is free. The first time you open the app, a screen full of prices appears, usually monthly or yearly, often with a free trial up front. Paying once and being done with it is rare.

There's a fair reason for that. An app needs years of upkeep, every new version of iOS means work, and a one-time purchase only pays once. If a company keeps your data on its own systems, it pays for that every month, and a subscription is often the right fit.

A habit tracker usually doesn't need any of that. At its core, it remembers which days you did something. Your iPhone can store that on its own, no account required. So with this kind of app, it's worth asking what exactly you'd be paying for each month.

How to spot a subscription before you download

If the button says Get instead of showing a price, only the download is free. What comes after that is listed elsewhere. Three places tell you.

  • Next to the Get button, it says In-App Purchases. So there's something to buy, but you don't know yet whether it's a subscription or a one-time purchase.
  • Further down the app page, under Information, you'll find In-App Purchases again. It lists every purchase with its price. Names like Monthly, Yearly or Premium (1 Year) mean a subscription.
  • If there's a single price with a name like Pro or Lifetime, it's usually a one-time purchase. You only know for sure once the app says so on its purchase screen.

Watch out for free trials in particular. Many subscriptions start with seven or 14 free days and then renew automatically. If you've started one, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. You can cancel it there too, ideally at least a day before the trial ends.

What a subscription costs over the years

A habit tracker isn't a one-month app. If it works, you'll use it for years, and that's exactly when the price starts to matter.

Here's an example with round numbers. A $3 monthly plan costs $36 a year and $108 after three years. A $30 yearly plan comes to $90 over the same period. A $5 one-time purchase stays at $5, however long you use it.

That doesn't make a subscription the wrong choice by default. If an app has features you really need and can't get anywhere else, it can be worth the money. Just do the math before the trial ends, not after.

There's one more thing people rarely think about. With some subscription apps, you only see part of your history after you cancel. If you've been tracking habits for three years, that's worth knowing before you sign up. The answer is usually in the app's help pages or FAQ.

Subscription vs. one-time purchase

Habit trackers with a subscription

  • Ongoing costs for as long as you use the app
  • A free trial that renews unless you cancel
  • Depending on the app, limited access to old entries after you cancel

Habit trackers you pay for once, or not at all

  • A fixed price you know upfront
  • Nothing to cancel and nothing that quietly renews
  • Purchases you can restore on a new iPhone with the same Apple Account
  • Often fewer features, since there's no recurring income

You don't have to see that last point as a downside. An app you pay for once tends to stick to what you downloaded it for. The more features pile up, the more of a chore the daily check-off becomes.

Checklist: how to find the right habit tracker

Price is only one question. A cheap tracker you stop opening after two weeks is still too expensive.

  • Check In-App Purchases for a subscription before you download.
  • Count how many steps a check-off takes. If it's more than two taps, you won't keep doing it every evening.
  • See if there's a widget. Checking off without opening the app makes it easier to stick with.
  • Think about what happens when you miss a day. If the app resets everything to zero, every slip feels like the end.
  • Ask yourself whether you want an account. A list of checkmarks rarely needs one.
  • Find out where your data lives: on your iPhone, in your iCloud or with the company behind the app.
  • Make sure you keep your old entries if you stop paying.

I deliberately left room for missed days in 12 Challenges. A challenge counts as done once you reach 85 % of the days, which is 26 out of 30. Why that works better than all or nothing is explained on the method page.

Can you do it without an app?

Yes. A wall calendar and a pen cost next to nothing and have worked for decades. Every day you get it done gets an X, and after a few days you won't want to break the chain.

A calendar has just three weak spots. It stays at home when you're out. It doesn't remind you at 21:00 that you haven't done anything yet. And with several goals at once, it gets messy fast.

A notes app or a spreadsheet solves the first problem, but not the other two. That's what a tracker is for, and it doesn't need a monthly fee to do it. If you're still looking for a goal, the challenge ideas library has 72 suggestions, each with a reason.

Questions about habit trackers with no subscription

Is there a habit tracker for iPhone with no subscription?

Yes. Alongside subscription apps, there are trackers that are completely free or sold as a one-time purchase. To see whether an app has a subscription, check In-App Purchases under Information on its App Store page. 12 Challenges is one of the apps without a subscription: the basics are free, and the optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase.

How can I tell if an app has a subscription?

On the app's App Store page, In-App Purchases under Information lists every price. Names like Monthly, Yearly or a duration in brackets point to a subscription. A single price with a name like Pro or Lifetime suggests a one-time purchase.

How much does 12 Challenges cost?

The download and the basics are free: creating and checking off challenges, reminders, syncing through your iCloud and the home screen widget. The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase of $4.99 in the US or £4.99 in the UK. The Supporter version costs $8.99 or £8.99. Both unlock the Apple Watch app, statistics and the full archive.

Does the 12 Challenges trial renew automatically?

No. The seven days of Pro start when you install the app and end on their own. After that, the app keeps running with its free features. There's no subscription that could renew and nothing you need to cancel.

How do I cancel an app subscription on iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. You'll see all active subscriptions there and can cancel each one separately. A subscription stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Cancel a free trial at least a day before it ends.

Do I keep a one-time purchase on a new iPhone?

Yes, as long as you use the same Apple Account. One-time purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored after you install the app on a new device. That includes the 12 Challenges Pro upgrade.

Do I need an account for 12 Challenges?

No. The app works without signing up or logging in. Your challenges are stored on your iPhone. If you like, the app can also keep them in your own iCloud.
